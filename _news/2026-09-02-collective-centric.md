Our new preprint [*Toward Collective-Centric Evaluation of Preference Inference for Participatory Democracy*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02990) is now available on arXiv! We propose a collective-centric evaluation framework to analyze how preference inference models affect the collective opinion landscape in participatory democracy platforms.
